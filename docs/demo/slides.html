The problem

Two satellites, two different counts.

  • MODIS (2000 →) and VIIRS (2012 →) see the same fires at different resolutions.
  • Joined naively, fire activity seems to jump in 2012: a change of instrument, not of burning.
  • MODIS is ending, so the record must continue on VIIRS.

“Is this month unusual for this place?” gets hard to answer.

FireCalendar India02
Our approach · the method, taken apart

Count cells, then calibrate.

  • Aqua MODIS and Suomi NPP VIIRS both pass at 13:30, so their counts compare fairly.
  • Unit: the fire cell-day. One burning cell on one day counts once, which removes most of the pixel-size gap.
  • Sparse blocks borrow strength from the national ratio; every factor has a 90% bootstrap interval.
  • Terra (2000–02) and NOAA-20 (2026 →) are chained in with their own ratios.

Who it helps

Built for people who act on fire.

  • Fire & disaster managers: is this month unusual? When does the season start here?
  • Scientists: a consistent 2000–present series, with raw counts and calibration factors kept.
  • Land & air-quality managers: Punjab's Oct–Nov paddy-residue burning, year by year.

Open & accessible: colour-blind-safe colours, every number printed, table views, CSV + PNG downloads, works on a phone. Presets open instantly.
